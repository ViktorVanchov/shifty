What is Shifty?+

Shifty is a platform that brings restaurants looking for staff together with people looking for restaurant work.

Who is it for?+

Both sides of the restaurant team: owners and managers who need staff, and people who want to work in restaurants.

Not quite yet. Registration forms for restaurants and workers will be added here soon.
